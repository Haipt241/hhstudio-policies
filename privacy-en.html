PRIVACY POLICY
Learn to Draw · Updated: October 28, 2026

Developer: H&H Studio — Hộ kinh doanh CNTT H&H, operator of Learn to Draw.
Privacy and support contact: pth241@gmail.com

1. Scope
This policy describes the current Android version of Learn to Draw, which provides drawing exercises, academic studies and drawing references made from selected photos.

2. Data on your device
The app stores language, audio and motion preferences, lesson progress, current steps, self-check marks and animation choices locally. When you choose a photo using the system picker, the app reads that photo and may keep an exercise copy in private app storage. It does not scan your photo library or access contacts, location or microphone.

3. Processing and sharing
Bundled models process photos on your device. This version does not transmit photos, progress, device identifiers or usage data to developer servers. It includes no advertising, behavioral analytics or cross-app tracking and requires no separate app account. Subscription payments, if available, are processed by Google Play using your store account; the app does not receive card numbers, your Google Play email or password. Personal data is not sold.

If you explicitly export or share an image using system features, it goes to the app or location you choose and the recipient's policies apply. If spoken guidance is enabled, the operating system's speech service may have its own policies; the app does not deliberately send photos to that service. If you email support, your email address and message are used to answer your request. Avoid including private images unless needed.

4. Retention and deletion
Local data remains until you delete it in the app, clear app storage in Android Settings or uninstall. Photo exercises can be deleted in Draw from photo. Clearing app storage removes progress and preferences and cannot be undone in the app. Delete exported copies separately at their destination. Android automatic backup is disabled. The developer has no server copy of your app data. Support correspondence is kept as needed to resolve your request; email the contact above to request deletion, subject to applicable retention requirements.

5. Control and protection
You choose which photo to open and can stop using photo features at any time. Internal data uses Android's private app storage protections; absolute security on every device cannot be guaranteed. Avoid sensitive images or other people's photos without permission.

6. Younger learners
The app does not ask for age and does not deliberately collect children's information over the network. Guardians should help select appropriate references and content. Bundled lessons can be used without personal photos.

7. Purchases and access
The app exchanges product/offer identifiers and purchase tokens with Google Play to purchase, restore, verify purchase signatures and acknowledge access. Purchase and access state is processed in memory during the app session. No developer server receives tokens in this version. The billing library and Google services may process connection information, transaction interactions and technical diagnostics for operation, fraud prevention and payment troubleshooting. Google handles account and payment information under its own policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy . Restoring a purchase does not synchronize lesson progress or create an app account.

8. Changes and contact
This policy will be updated when data practices change. The date appears above. Send privacy questions, requests or concerns to pth241@gmail.com.
