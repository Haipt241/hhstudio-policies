PRIVACY POLICY
Learn to Draw · Updated: November 26, 2026

Developer: H&H Studio — Hộ kinh doanh CNTT H&H, operator of Learn to Draw.
Privacy and support contact: admin@hhitpro.com

1. Scope
This policy describes the current Android version of Learn to Draw, which provides drawing exercises, academic studies and drawing references made from selected photos.

2. Data on your device
The app stores language, audio and motion preferences, lesson progress, current steps, self-check marks and animation choices locally. When you choose a photo using the system picker, the app reads that photo and may keep an exercise copy in private app storage. It does not scan your photo library or access contacts, location or microphone.

Saved drawings are kept as PNG images in the app’s private collection, with an artwork identifier, title, dimensions, creation time and last modification time. Drawing-tool settings and your chosen save destination are also stored locally. From version 1.2.6 (build 23), Save replaces the saved image for the current drawing session; Save as copy creates a separate item.

3. Processing and sharing
Bundled models process photos on your device. This version does not transmit photos, progress, device identifiers or usage data to developer servers. It includes no advertising, behavioral analytics or cross-app tracking and requires no separate app account. Subscription payments, if available, are processed by Google Play using your store account; the app does not receive card numbers, your Google Play email or password. Personal data is not sold.

If you explicitly export or share an image using system features, it goes to the app or location you choose and the recipient's policies apply. If spoken guidance is enabled, the operating system's speech service may have its own policies; the app does not deliberately send photos to that service. If you email support, your email address and message are used to answer your request. Avoid including private images unless needed.

Saving to the device photo gallery is optional and follows your choice for each save or the preference you explicitly ask the app to remember. You can change this in My collection → Save options. Exports contain the drawing on white paper, without the reference photo or guide lines. Other apps with access to your gallery, including a cloud-photo backup service you enable, may process those exported files under their own policies.

4. Retention and deletion
You can delete saved drawings in My collection and photo exercises in Draw from photo. Save replaces the previous saved image for that drawing session; use Save as copy first to keep a separate version. Deleting an app copy does not delete an image already exported to the device gallery or another destination. Delete those copies separately, including any copies held by a backup service you use. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes the private collection, photo exercises, progress and preferences. Android automatic backup of app data is disabled, and the developer has no server copy to restore. Support correspondence is kept as needed to resolve your request; email the contact above to request deletion, subject to applicable retention requirements.

5. Control and protection
You choose which photo to open and can stop using photo features at any time. Internal data uses Android's private app storage protections; absolute security on every device cannot be guaranteed. Avoid sensitive images or other people's photos without permission.

On Android 7–9, the app requests storage-write permission only when you choose to export to the device gallery. On Android 10 and later, it saves app-created images without requesting broad access to your photo library. Saving only inside the app does not need that storage permission.

6. Age ranges and younger learners
From version 1.2.7, the app uses Google Play Age Signals to receive an age range, its signal source and parental approval status for app changes when supplied by Google Play. Google manages age sharing, verification and supervision; the app does not ask for an exact birth date, identity documents or verification photos. Signals are processed in session memory, are not sent to developer servers and are not used for advertising, marketing, profiling or behavioral analytics. The app does not store or use the installation identifier or approval date that the API may return.

Only flags indicating whether age sharing is mandatory and which verification, sharing or approval action remains required are stored locally, so a network error or restart cannot bypass a known requirement. Age ranges, birth dates and identity documents are not stored. Flags are updated from new Google Play responses and removed when app data is cleared.

Where sharing is optional, declining to share or having an unknown age still allows the drawing experience appropriate for all ages; an unknown age is not treated as adulthood. When Play confirms that verification, mandatory sharing or parental approval of an app change is required, the app asks you to complete this in Google Play before continuing, including Basic. Younger or supervised learners are reminded to ask a parent to review purchase terms. The app proactively requests a review only when the signal indicates an unsupervised user aged 18 or older. Guardians should help choose appropriate photos. Google’s privacy policy applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy .

7. Purchases and access
The app uses product and offer identifiers and purchase tokens when communicating with Google Play to make and restore purchases and acknowledge subscriptions. Purchase signatures are verified on your device. Purchase and access state is processed in memory during the app session. No developer server receives tokens in this version. The billing library and Google services may process connection information, transaction interactions and technical diagnostics for operation, fraud prevention and payment troubleshooting. Google handles account and payment information under its own policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy . Restoring a purchase does not synchronize lesson progress or create an app account.

The app also obtains the Google Play country code to display regional pricing; it does not obtain your GPS location. Basic does not require a payment method or a subscription. Google Play may still be contacted for available prices, existing purchases and app updates when using Basic.

8. Optional Google Play reviews
To time review invitations and limit reminders, the app stores only on your device up to four identifiers for artworks viewed or used for practice and the date of the latest review request attempt. These identifiers contain no photos and are not sent to Google Play or developer servers.

You may voluntarily submit a star rating and comment through Google Play. Submissions go directly to Google Play over an encrypted connection and may appear publicly; in a closed test, they are shared privately with the developer. The app does not receive your rating, comment or confirmation that you submitted a review. This feature uses no developer server. You can delete reviews through your Google Play Store account or Google Account. Google's policy applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy .

9. Google Play app updates
From version 1.2.6, the app checks Google Play for available updates. Google Play processes device metadata, the app version and the list of installed modules and asset packs to determine update availability and download size. Google states that this data is encrypted, is not transferred to third parties and is deleted after a fixed retention period. No developer server receives it. The app stores the required update version locally. Once a required update is confirmed, learning is blocked until the update is installed; an unknown or offline check can allow access when no required update is already known. Google’s privacy policy applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy .

10. Public policy and support pages
Our public policy and support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, including visits without a GitHub account. Its privacy statement applies: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement . Reading the policies bundled inside the app does not open these web pages.

11. Changes and contact
This policy will be updated when data practices change. The date appears above. Send privacy questions, requests or concerns to admin@hhitpro.com.
